# CONJURE: MASTER GLOSSARY & TERMINOLOGY

## I. Core Entities & Combat Roles
* **The Hero:** The primary player avatar. Operates as the frontline combatant who utilizes baseline physical attacks, obstacle constructs, and displacement spells.
* **The Companion:** An untargetable secondary caster selected prior to a run. They stand off-grid alongside the Hero, determining the specialized elemental pool, runic synergies, and finisher spells available for the deck. 
* **The Pact:** The shared health pool (base 40 HP) representing the mystical bond and tether between the Hero and their Companion. Combat ends if The Pact is broken (HP reduced to 0).
* **Minion:** Standard summoned units that occupy exactly a $1 \times 1$ grid cell on the tactical board.
* **Behemoth:** High-cost, tactical centerpiece units that occupy a massive $2 \times 2$ grid footprint. They naturally possess the *Heavy Footprint* trait.
* **Wandering Duelist:** Humanoid NPCs that roam Open World safe zones. They can be challenged to standard skirmishes or high-stakes *Ante Wagers*.
* **Apex Boss:** Legendary, multi-phase continental figures that guard Sanctums and yield *Marrow* upon defeat.

## II. Resources, Economy & Progression
* **Pips (Banked Pips):** The persistent, baseline magical resource generated each turn (+1 per round). Unused Pips roll over across turns into the Pip Bank (capped at 8 Pips).
* **Echo:** Volatile, fleeting energy generated dynamically during a turn (via unit sacrifices, Devour mechanics, or combat loops). Echoes act as temporary Pips but strictly expire into nothingness at the end of the round.
* **Ducats (DUC):** The primary liquid currency of the world. Used to pay for Ante buybacks, ferry tolls, scribing materials (parchment/ink), and vendor booster packs. 
* **Academy Crests (AC):** A prestige gate currency earned from school mastery trials and Subjugation rituals. Used to unlock specialized school blueprints and relic sockets.
* **Marrow:** The visceral, hard-bottleneck upgrade currency extracted from Apex Bosses and disenchanted magic. It is used to breathe ultimate life into cards (Ascending them to Rank 2) and synthesize Behemoth Heartstones.
* **Renown:** A regional reputation meter (Tiered as Novice, Adept, and Master). Upgrading Renown unlocks higher-stakes Ante Wagers, district access, and advanced masteries on specific continents.
* **Subjugation Trial:** A specialized boss encounter in the open world where the player battles a wild Companion directly. Fulfilling mastery sub-objectives during the trial improves the Companion's variance roll upon capture.
* **Ante Wagering:** A high-stakes duel format where players wager Ducats and physical cards from their active binder against Wandering Duelists. Lost cards can be reclaimed via a Magistracy bounty fee within 24 hours.

## III. Card Types, Crafting & Equipment
* **Card Classifications:** Cards are strictly typed as Attack (physical/melee), Skill (utility/buffs), Spell (elemental magic), Construct (obstacles), Rune (delayed traps), or Reaction.
* **Power Tier (Tier 3):** Climax cards representing the absolute peak of a deck's power. They are hard-capped at exactly 1 copy per deck and their casting cost cannot be discounted below 1 Pip.
* **Ephemeral Overlay:** A temporary, 0-Pip card (like the *Rite of Binding*) generated by specific boss/event triggers. It attaches directly to the HUD, temporarily pushing the hand limit from 8 to 9, and cannot be discarded.
* **Scribing Blueprints:** Recipes discovered in the open world or dungeons that allow players to craft new minion/spell cards at a campfire using reagents and Ducats.
* **Ascension (Rank 1 / Rank 2):** The process of spending *Marrow* to permanently rewrite a card's parameters, pushing it from Rank 1 to Rank 2 to enhance geometry, durability, or keyword efficiency.
* **Relic Artifacts:** Equippable, persistent items for the Hero that passively modify macro-combat rules (e.g., altering hand size, Pip generation, or starting armor). 

## IV. The Tactical Grid & Environment
* **The Runic Boundary:** The glowing, magical perimeter (or physical lip) surrounding the battle grid. Units shoved off the edge of the board hit this boundary, taking maximum collision damage ($3/2$) instead of falling into the void.
* **Safe Zones:** Urban avenues, paved roads, and well-lit sanctuaries where enemies cannot aggro and combat cannot be initiated.
* **Danger Zones:** Unpaved alleys, murky waterways, or wild terrains where real-time enemy aggression and field hazard harvesting take place.
* **Line-of-Sight (LoS) Shadows:** A physical, ray-casted cone of occlusion behind obstacles and Behemoths that cleanly blocks ranged targeting for those caught within it.

## V. Universal Combat & Timing Keywords
* **Attuned:** Triggers a secondary, amplified card effect if an allied Companion matching the designated magic school is currently active.
* **Resonance:** A continuous passive field engine that triggers automatically whenever a spell matching the active Companion's school is played.
* **Fast:** A card that can be played during reaction windows, opponent's turns, or chained instantly without passing action priority.
* **Retain:** Prevents a card from being discarded at the end of the round.
* **Cycle:** Discard a specified number of cards, then draw that exact same number from the deck.
* **Channel:** Converts 1 unspent Pip into +2 Echoes for the following turn.
* **Dormant (X):** The unit/construct enters the board inactive, petrified, and invulnerable for X number of turns before awakening.
* **Exhaust:** Banishes a played card for the remainder of the dungeon instance or battle.
* **Cascade:** An explosive chain reaction caused when a detonating Rune sets off adjacent overlapping Runes.
* **Last Stand:** A sudden-death mechanic triggered when The Pact reaches critical damage, where ambient audio muffles and the game enters a high-stakes panic state.

## VI. Spatial & Displacement Keywords
* **Shove (X):** Forces a target unit violently backward X tiles along the vector of the attack. 
* **Pull (X):** Drags a target unit X tiles toward the caster along a straight line.
* **Guardian:** Redirects incoming single-target ranged attacks away from adjacent allies, forcing the attack to strike the Guardian unit instead.
* **Cleave:** Strikes a primary target as well as the two orthogonal tiles adjacent to it (T-shaped or cone AoE).
* **Pierce:** Ignores target Persistent Armor to deal true damage directly to the base HP pool / The Pact.
* **Counter:** Strikes back for flat damage the first time the unit is hit by an adjacent melee attack in a round.
* **Escalate:** Grants permanent stat increases (+ATK or +HP) to a unit whenever a specified tactical condition is met (capped at +3 baseline growth).
* **Haste:** Allows a unit to move and attack on the exact same turn it is summoned.
* **Stealth:** Prevents a unit from being targeted by direct attacks or spells until it breaks cover by executing an attack.
* **Heavy Footprint:** An inherent Behemoth trait that grants total immunity to standard *Shove* and *Pull* mechanics.

## VII. The Elemental Schools & Specific Statuses

### 1. Pyre
*School of volatile combustion, hellfire, and aggressive AoE cleaves.*
* **Ignite:** Applies stacks of Burn that deal damage automatically at the start of the unit's turn.
* **Combust:** Instantly strips all remaining Burn stacks on a target, converting them into a massive, single-hit detonation.

### 2. Frost
*School of fortified ice barricades, Line-of-Sight shadows, and crowd control.*
* **Chill:** A debuff that reduces unit Movement (MOV) and acts as a prerequisite status for freezing.
* **Freeze:** Completely immobilizes a unit for a set duration.
* **Shatter:** Consumes a *Freeze* status to deal true damage and instantly strip a target of all Persistent Armor.
* **Brittle:** Applied alongside Chill; causes units adjacent to the Brittle target to become vulnerable to AoE splash damage.

### 3. Surge
*School of kinetic voltage, high momentum, and electrical chains.*
* **Arc (X):** Deals a percentage (usually 50%) of the primary strike's damage to X adjacent enemy units.
* **Overload:** Unlocks bonus card parameters/damage if it is the 3rd (or subsequent) card played in a single turn.

### 4. Bulwark
*School of tectonic mass, physical barricades, and heavy displacement.*
* **Persistent Armor:** Defensive shielding that carries over between rounds and does not decay until broken via damage.
* **Fortify:** The action of granting Persistent Armor to an ally or obstacle.
* **Concuss:** Causes physical collision damage against walls/units to scale upward based on 50% of the user's current Persistent Armor.

### 5. Dusk
*School of shadow rites, occult sacrifices, and Echo generation.*
* **Devour:** Allows a unit to be summoned directly on top of an allied minion. It destroys the ally, absorbing 100% of its remaining stats as Temporary Armor, and refunds Echoes.
* **Hollow:** A death-trigger. Grants Echoes to the player on the following turn if the unit dies or is sacrificed.
* **Frail:** A curse debuff that permanently increases all incoming damage taken by the afflicted unit.

### 6. Bloom
*School of toxic spores, creeping root traps, and vitality leeching.*
* **Toxin:** Bypasses Persistent Armor completely to deal true damage directly to unit HP over time.
* **Entangle:** Drops a unit's Movement (MOV) down to exactly 0 for the turn.
* **Leech:** Restores HP to the player's lowest-health active unit/Pact whenever the afflicted enemy target takes damage.

### 7. Arcane
*The neutral, foundational magic discipline representing martial prowess, physical manipulation, and general deck cycling utility. Accessible to all decks regardless of Companion alignment.*
