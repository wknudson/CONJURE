# Tactical Card Battler: Master Combat Architecture (Draft 7.0)

## 1. System Philosophy & Influences
Draft 7.0 formalizes the complete combat and deck-building mechanics for a deterministic, grid-based card battler. The game relies on strict spatial geometry, non-decaying resource layers, and high-visibility board states.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CORE INFLUENCE MATRIX                           │
├───────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Wizard101         │ Pip Banking, Round-Ramp, Attached Runes/Wards      │
│ Hearthstone       │ Board Trading, Off-Grid Portrait Target System     │
│ Inscryption       │ Unit Economy, In-Turn Sparks & Devour Spawning     │
│ Pirate101/Tactics │ Grid Movement, Line of Sight, Spatial Obstacles   │
│ Slay the Spire    │ Tight 15-Card Decks, Retain Engine, Free Reshuffle │
└───────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Command Architecture & Dual-Entity Dynamic

### 2.1 The Off-Grid Commander Interface
Neither the player's Commander nor the enemy Boss occupies a tile on the physical combat grid. Both entities exist as off-grid portrait anchors positioned on their respective side of the arena.

```
+========================================================================+
|                      [ ENEMY HERO / BOSS PORTRAIT ]                    |
+========================================================================+
|  Row 5 [Enemy Backline]    :  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]        |
|  Row 4 [Enemy Forward]     :  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]        |
|  Row 3 [Neutral Clash]     :  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]        |
|  Row 2 [Player Forward]    :  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]        |
|  Row 1 [Player Backline]   :  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]  [ . ]        |
+========================================================================+
|     [ PLAYER HERO PORTRAIT ]      |     [ COMPANION FAMILIAR ]         |
|     (Shared Pool: 40 HP)          |     (Untargetable Spellcaster)     |
+========================================================================+
```

* **Shared Life Anchor:** The Hero and Companion share a single pool of **40 HP**. Direct damage delivered to the portrait directly reduces this pool.
* **Untargetable Companion:** The Companion stands beside the Hero, functioning as a non-targetable caster. It is selected prior to the run and determines the specialized elemental/mechanical pool for that run.
* **Card Distribution:**
  * **Hero Cards:** Baseline spells, physical attacks, displacement cards, shield buffs, and obstacle constructs.
  * **Companion Cards:** Elemental Runes, high-cost catalysts, sacrifice synergies, and finisher spells.

## 3. Resource Engine: Pips, Overflow, and Temporary Sparks

The game features an asymmetrical resource model separating persistent mana (Pips) from burst, turn-only mana (Sparks).

```
[Start of Turn: +1 Regular Pip] ──> [Reserve Bank (Cap: 8)]
                                           │
[Minion Sacrifice / Offering]   ──> [Temporary Sparks] ──> [Combined Pool] ──> [Spend on Cards]
                                           │                       │
                                           │            (End of Turn: Discard Sparks)
                                           └──────────> (Cap Reserves down to 8)
```

### 3.1 Pips (Persistent Mana)
* **Generation:** $+1\text{ Regular Pip}$ is granted at the start of every player turn.
* **Banking:** Unused Pips roll over across turns into the Pip Bank.
* **End-of-Turn Cap:** The Pip Bank has a strict limit of **8 Pips** during the cleanup phase.

### 3.2 Temporary Sparks (Sacrifice Mana)
* **Generation:** Generated by sacrificing friendly minions, devouring board assets, or casting specialized offering cards.
* **Turn-Only Lifespan:** Sparks do not roll over across rounds and expire upon turn end.
* **In-Turn Overflow:** During a turn, the combined Pip + Spark total can exceed 8 without penalty. The hard cap of 8 is only enforced during the End of Turn Phase.

## 4. Grid Geometry, Deployment, and Movement Mechanics

### 4.1 Arena Layout
* **Dimensions:** Variable by encounter ($5 \times 5$, $4 \times 6$, etc.).
* **Territory Designation:**
  * *Player Territory:* Rows 1 and 2.
  * *Neutral Clash Zone:* Row 3.
  * *Enemy Territory:* Rows 4 and 5.

### 4.2 Spawning & Deployment
* **Standard Summon:** Deployed onto any unoccupied tile within Player Territory.
* **Devour / Overwrite Spawning:** A minion can be cast directly on top of an active friendly minion.
  * The existing minion is consumed, immediately contributing its sacrifice Spark value to the new card's cost.
  * Any Rune attached to the consumed minion **fizzles and is discarded** without triggering, unless the consumed minion has a specific "On-Devour" trait.
  * Accumulated Escalation stats do not carry over by default.
* **Territory Lockdown:** If all tiles in Player Territory are occupied by enemy units or obstacles, standard summoning is disabled until space is cleared, unless the card explicitly possesses an "Enemy Crush" deployment trait.

### 4.3 Movement and Action Restrictions
* **Movement Vectors:** Minions can move orthogonally and diagonally up to their card-defined `MOV` value.
* **Strict Commitment (No Split Movement):** A minion may perform a **Move-then-Attack** or an **Attack-then-Move**, but **cannot** perform a Move-Attack-Move sequence. Declaring an attack after moving immediately exhausts the unit.
* **Large Footprints ($2 \times 2$ Behemoths):**
  * Behemoths occupy 4 grid tiles.
  * A $2 \times 2$ unit requires an open $2 \times 2$ pathway and **cannot** squeeze through $1 \times 1$ openings.

## 5. Collision Physics & Line of Sight (LoS)

### 5.1 Displacement and Collision Rules
When a target is pushed or displaced (e.g., via *Shield Bash*), collisions resolve instantly using standardized damage:

```
[Attacker Displaces Target] 
       │
       ├──> Hits Wall / Arena Border   ──> Target takes 3 Impact Damage
       │
       ├──> Hits Obstructing Unit      ──> Target takes 3 Impact Damage; Blocker takes 2 Collateral Damage
       │
       └──> Hits Destructible Obstacle ──> Target takes 3 Impact Damage; Obstacle takes 3 Damage
```

* *Mass Invariance:* Collision damage remains standard ($3 / 2$) regardless of whether the blocker is a $1 \times 1$ minion or a $2 \times 2$ Behemoth.

### 5.2 Portrait Targeting, Guardians, and Vision Occlusion
* **Melee Range (Range 1–2):** Must reach the enemy's front or back row (Row 4 or 5) to target the off-board Boss portrait directly.
* **Ranged Range (Range 3+):** Can strike the portrait from a distance if a straight or diagonal vector exists.
* **Cone Line of Sight (LoS) Occlusion:** Destructible obstacles, fog/smoke hazards, and units with the **Guardian** trait project a vision shadow cone behind them, blocking direct line-of-sight and diagonal angles to the portrait.

```
[Enemy Front]       [ . ]         [ . ]         [ . ]
                      │             │             │
[Midfield]          [ . ]   [ GUARDIAN UNIT ]   [ . ]
                             /      │      \
[Player Backline]     [ X ]       [ X ]     [ X ]   <── (OCCLUDED CONE TILES)
```

## 6. Combat Resolution, Armor, and Status Mechanics

### 6.1 Attack Mechanics & Counter
* **One-Sided Trades:** Standard physical melee strikes do not cause simultaneous retaliation.
* **Counter (Riposte):** Only units with the explicit **Counter** keyword strike back with their current ATK value when hit by a melee attack.

### 6.2 Persistent Armor vs. Health Penetration
* **Persistent Lifespan:** Armor does not decay at the end of the turn. It stacks and remains on the unit/Hero until stripped by damage.
* **Rune Trigger Penetration Rule:** Runes with damage-based triggers (e.g., *Cinder Rune*) do **not** detonate if all incoming damage is absorbed by Armor. Damage must penetrate Armor and cause **at least 1 point of actual HP loss** to activate the Rune.

### 6.3 Status Conditions & Incapacitation
* **Turn-Start Resolution:** Recurring status effects (Burn, Poison, Bleed) tick at the **start of the afflicted unit's turn**.
* **Stacking Intensity:** Applying an existing status effect increases its stack count (magnifying tick damage) rather than refreshing duration.
* **Incapacitation (Stun / Freeze):** Prevents movement and attack declarations, but does **not** stop the unit from receiving turn-start Escalation buffs.

### 6.4 Threat Engine: Minion Escalation
* **Anti-Face Pressure:** Surviving friendly minions scale at the **start of your turn** (gaining $+1/+1$, $+2\text{ ATK}$, or generating bonus Pips/Sparks) if they survived the enemy round.
* Minions do not escalate on the exact turn they are deployed.

## 7. Runes: Cascades, Limits, and Fizzle Conditions

```
[Cast Rune onto Unit / Obstacle] (Limit: 1 per Entity)
              │
              ├──> [Trigger Met via Raw HP Damage / Death] ──> [Detonation: AoE Damage]
              │                                                        │
              │                                                        └──> [Hits Adjacent Rune] ──> [CASCADE CHAIN]
              │
              └──> [Killed by Wrong Damage Type or Devoured]  ──> [Rune Fizzles: No Detonation]
```

1. **Attachment Limit:** A target (minion or obstacle) can hold a maximum of **1 attached Rune** at any given time.
2. **Chain Reaction (Cascade):** If Rune A explodes and deals elemental damage that penetrates the armor of an adjacent unit holding Rune B, Rune B detonates immediately within the same resolution step.
3. **Fizzle Rule:** If an entity with an attached Rune is destroyed by an unaligned damage source (e.g., Physical damage against a Fire Rune), the Rune **fizzles and is discarded** without detonating.

## 8. Complete Turn Flow Sequence

```
===================================================================================
1. START OF TURN PHASE
   - Gain +1 Regular Pip (Rolls into Bank, max 8)
   - Draw 4 Cards from Deck (Reshuffle discard if empty without fatigue)
   - Tick active Status Conditions (Burn / Poison)
   - Trigger Escalation on all surviving friendly minions
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ACTION PHASE (Player Turn)
   - Move active units (Up to MOV limit; orthogonal/diagonal)
   - Play Hero & Companion cards using Pips/Sparks
   - Resolve Unit Readiness:
       * Dormant: No action on turn played
       * Impact: Immediate on-play trigger resolves
       * Haste: May move and attack immediately
   - Declare Attacks against enemy units, obstacles, or Boss portrait
   - Sacrifice un-exhausted units to generate Temporary Sparks
   - Devour/Overwrite friendly units to spawn new minions
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DETONATION & RESOLUTION PHASE
   - Evaluate all Rune trigger conditions
   - Resolve single detonations and multi-tile Cascade Chains
   - Apply collision damage from displacement spells
   - Check for lethal conditions
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. END OF TURN PHASE (Cleanup)
   - Discard unplayed non-Retained cards
   - Expire all remaining Temporary Sparks
   - Cap Pip Bank down to 8 Pips
   - Hand turn over to Enemy AI
===================================================================================
```

## 9. Tie-Breakers: "Last Stand" Sudden Death
If an AoE blast or cascade reaction reduces both the Player's Shared HP and the Enemy Boss's HP to 0 in the exact same trigger step:
* Both entities revive immediately with **1 HP**.
* All non-obstacle grid units and active Runes are wiped from the board.
* Combat resumes in sudden death until a decisive winner is established.
