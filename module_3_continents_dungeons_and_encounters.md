## 1. High-Level World & Progression Architecture

Rather than isolated single-element biome spheres, **CONJURE** is structured around vast, culturally complex **Continents**. Elemental schools are not segregated by nature; instead, societies, guilds, and ancient ruins harness multiple schools in their technology, infrastructure, governance, and combat doctrines.

Progression follows a rich, mystery-driven investigation loop: the Hero arrives in a continental capital, uncovers local conspiracies and arcane disturbances, solves environmental glyph puzzles, navigates perilous street corridors, collects forbidden scribing blueprints, challenges wandering duelists under strict Ante rules, and conquers multi-floor dungeon sanctums to bind legendary Companions.

### Core World Rules
1. **The Sidewalk Immunity Rule:** All urban avenues, paved roads, sanctuary courtyards, and well-lit walkways are designated **Safe Zones**. Enemies cannot aggro or initiate combat while the Hero is on a sidewalk.
2. **The Danger Zone:** Stepping off the pavement onto open cobblestones, unpaved alleyways, murky waterways, or overgrown courtyards instantly enables real-time proximity detection and hostile aggro cones.
3. **Investigation-Driven Progress:** Story chapters advance through dialogue interrogation, crime-scene glyph sifting, and blueprint discovery rather than blind mob grinding.
4. **Persistent Multi-Room Attrition:** Dungeons are structured as vertical multi-floor sanctums where Commander HP (40 HP base) carries over across all floors without automated healing.

### 2.1 Continent 1: The Crownreach Archonate
* **Visual & Cultural Identity:** Sprawling neo-gothic metropolis powered by clockwork leylines, gaslit brick thoroughfares, grand scholastic spires, and labyrinthine canal catacombs.
* **Dominant Elemental Factions:** Arcane/Aether (High Magistracy), Surge (Industrial Machinists), Dusk (Black-Market Scribes).
* **The Overarching Mystery:** The central Great Clockwork Engine is running backward, infusing the lower wards with temporal rifts and turning citizens into hollow crystalline thralls. The player must trace smuggled forbidden Dusk scrolls from the black markets to the Grand Magistracy.
* **Zonal Breakdown:**
  * **The Grand Plaza (Safe Hub):** High Magistracy Chambers, Master Scribe Guild, card binder vaults, and Ante wagering parlors.
  * **Iron-Gutter Ward (Mid-Tier Street Danger):** Narrow alleys patrolled by rogue clockwork automata and syndicate rogues utilizing Bulwark bludgeons and Surge shock-whips.
  * **The Sunless Catacombs (Subterranean Gauntlet):** Flooded, dark crypts containing forgotten royal vaults and corrupted elemental runes.
* **Climactic Apex Dungeon:** *The Chrono-Spire Citadel* (4 Floors).
* **Continental Boss:** **Arch-Magister Vane & The Clockwork Colossus** (Surge/Aether hybrid).
* **Companion Covenant:** **Ignis (Pyre Salamander)** — Purified and bound after the player disables the rogue thermal exhaust core in the Spire basement.

* **Visual & Cultural Identity:** Endless vitrified glass dunes, brass sun-forges, astronomical canyon observatories, and submerged palaces swallowed by golden sands.
* **Dominant Elemental Factions:** Surge (Astral Astrologers), Pyre (Solar Forgers), Bulwark (Sandstone Masons).

  * **The Gilded Oasis (Safe Hub):** Silk-tented bazaar, wandering camel caravans, blueprint scribes, and duelist wager rings.

  * **The Sunken Necropolis (Exploration Dungeon):** Half-buried brass tombs filled with mirror-reflection beam puzzles and heavy stone sentinels.
* **Climactic Apex Dungeon:** *The Vault of Thousand Mirrors* (4 Floors).
* **Continental Boss:** **Grand Astrologer Zahir & The Solar Pylon Engine** (Surge/Pyre hybrid).
* **Companion Covenant:** **Volt (Thunder Falcon)** — Bound atop the highest solar observatory after re-routing the planetary lightning conduit.

* **Visual & Cultural Identity:** Monolithic black iron fortresses built directly into active volcanic calderas, glowing molten sluices, massive steam pistons, and ash-choked canyons.

### 3.1 The Sidewalk Boundary System
* **Curbside Raycasting:** The player avatar constantly checks ground surface tags (`Surface_SafeWalkway` vs `Surface_DangerField`).
* **Safe Walkway Privileges:** While on a safe walkway, mob vision cones and aggro rings are completely suppressed. The player can walk directly past dangerous mobs without initiating combat.
* **Danger Zone Transition:** Entering the danger zone enables full AI perception.

### 3.2 Dynamic Combat Circles & Multi-Enemy Pulls
When collision occurs in the danger zone, a 5-meter glowing **Combat Ring** expands from the point of contact for 2.5 seconds:
1. **Primary Encounter:** The colliding mob spawns on Rank 5 of the tactical combat grid.
2. **Reinforcement Pulls (Multi-Enemy):** Any roaming mob that touches the expanding ring during the 2.5s window is drawn into the battle:
   * **Wave 2 Deployment:** Reinforcements enter the battle at the start of Round 2, deploying on Rank 6.
   * **Pip Compensation:** For every additional mob pulled into combat, the player receives +1 Banked Pip and +1 Card Draw at the start of Round 2 to balance action economy.

### 3.3 Contact Advantage Modifiers

| Contact Vector | Advantage State | Tactical Grid Modifiers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frontal Collision** | Neutral State | Standard starting resources (3 Pips, 5 Cards, symmetric deployment). |
| **Rear Ambush (Player Backstab)** | Player Advantage | Player starts with +1 Banked Pip (4 total), draws 6 cards, and gains Turn 1 Priority. |
| **Cornered Ambush (Enemy Rear Strike)** | Enemy Advantage | Enemy spawns with +1 Vanguard Footman pre-deployed on Row 3; Player starts with 2 Pips on Turn 1. |

## 4. Investigation Systems, Arcane Clues & Scribing Discovery

Questing in **CONJURE** prioritizes active deduction, crime-scene analysis, and intellectual puzzle-solving over repetitive mob farming.

```

├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                              │

│                                                   │                          │

│                                                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.1 Clue Sifting & Residue Analysis
* **Arcane Residue Nodes:** Interactive investigation props located in crime scenes or ruin chambers. Interacting triggers an elemental spectral lens:
  * *Pyre Residue:* Reveals thermal footprints showing where a suspect fled.
  * *Dusk Residue:* Summons a temporary echo of a deceased NPC to recount their final dialogue.
  * *Surge Residue:* Recharges broken automata mechanisms to unlock encrypted memory cores.

### 4.2 Runic Glyph Alignment Mini-Game
Sealed dungeon gates, private study vaults, and relic chests are locked by **Runic Glyph Matrices** ($3 	imes 3$ grid of elemental sigils):
* **Puzzle Rules:** Players rotate rows and columns to connect continuous elemental energy lines from the source pylon to the anchor lock.
* **Failure State:** Tripping a security glyph triggers a localized trap battle with 2x environmental hazard obstacles.

### 4.3 Scribing Blueprint Hunting
Finished cards are rarely handed out directly; players discover **Scribing Blueprints** that teach permanent recipes in their Spellbook:
* **Master Scribe Encounters:** Hidden in secluded district nooks; teach specialized school recipes upon reaching specific Renown tiers.
* **Prop Dismantling:** Harvesting interactive combat props (e.g., Volatile Casks, Rime Spires) using crafting tools permanently unlocks their corresponding Construct cards.
* **Ante Wagering Victories:** Defeating elite wandering duelists in taverns awards rare blueprint scrolls directly from their personal collection.

### 5.1 Dungeon Attrition & Endurance Rules
1. **Persistent Commander Health:** Commander HP (40 HP base) does not reset between floors. Damage taken in Floor 1 carries directly into Floor 2, 3, and 4.
2. **Exhausted Card Locks:** Cards played with the **Exhaust** keyword remain banished for the remainder of the dungeon instance.
3. **Fountain of Reconstitution (Floor 2 Rest Sanctuary):** Grants the player exactly one strategic boon:
   * **Drink:** Restore 20 lost Commander HP.
   * **Meditate:** Upgrade one selected card in active deck to its Rank 2 variant for the remainder of the run.
   * **Attune:** Start the Floor 4 Boss battle with +2 permanent Banked Pips.

### 5.2 Floor Mutator Catalog

| Mutator Name | Visual Profile | Mechanical Impact on Combat Grid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aetheric Miasma** | Purple shimmering haze | All Spells cost +1 Temporary Spark unless the caster is on a clean, hazard-free cell. |
| **Slick Permafrost** | Translucent ice floor | Units knocked backward slide indefinitely until impacting a wall or obstacle (+2 slide damage). |
| **Slag Cascade** | Lava drips from ceiling | Every 2 rounds, 3 random grid tiles are struck by falling lava, turning them into Burning tiles (2 fire dmg). |
| **Conductive Floor** | Flooded shallow water | Water tiles conduct all Surge and Shock damage across entire contiguous pools. |
| **Tectonic Tremors** | Shaking cavern ceiling | Wall and unit collision damage is doubled (6 damage for walls, 4 damage for unit-on-unit collisions). |

## 7. Continental Renown, Wandering Duelists & Wagering Economy

Each continent maintains a standalone **Renown Meter** (0–2000 Points). Advancing Renown tiers unlocks district access, advanced masteries, and higher-stakes wagering matches.

### 7.1 Wandering Duelist Ante Mechanics
* Humanoid duelists wander the safe plazas and taverns of each continent.
* Interacting allows the player to initiate an **Ante Wagering Match**:
  * **Novice Tier:** Wager 50–100 Gold.
  * **Adept Tier:** Wager 250 Gold + 1 Tier 2 Card from active Binder.
  * **Master Tier:** Wager 500 Gold + 1 Tier 3 Power Card.
* **Defeat Forfeit:** Losing forfeits the wagered card to the Duelist. Players can reclaim lost cards by paying a Reclaim Bounty at the Continental Magistracy within 24 hours.
